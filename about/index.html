Who we are

Wellness that speaks every South African language.

Sinoluvo Consulting offers customised and holistic wellness solutions and training across South Africa. We take pride in tailoring our services to the South African context by integrating cultural elements where needed, allowing us to accommodate the diverse cultures of our nation.

Our interventions are informed by the Health Belief and Behavioural Change Models. Through tailored health and wellness interventions, specialised workplace interventions and training, we empower individuals to take ownership of their careers, health and wellbeing — resulting in happier and more fulfilling lives.
