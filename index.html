Who we are

Holistic wellness, rooted in the South African context.

Sinoluvo Consulting is a 100% black-owned Employee Health and Wellness provider, offering customised and holistic wellness solutions and training across South Africa.

We tailor every service to the South African context, integrating cultural elements where needed so that we can serve the diverse cultures of our nation — all guided by the spirit of uBuntu.
